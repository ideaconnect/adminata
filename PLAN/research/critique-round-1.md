## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

The eight reports are unusually deep and, taken together, cover every one of the 131 templates, the PHP coupling surface, the packaging model and the TailAdmin catalogue. They are **not yet an executable plan** for three reasons: (1) they disagree on the JS runtime, jQuery, naming, defaults, dark-mode storage, scroll model, datepicker ownership and the CSS compat-layer mechanism, and several of those disagreements have technical consequences (a `@utility`-based compat layer would be purged from the prebuilt CSS; reusing the `datepicker` Stimulus identifier silently loses to form-extensions); (2) a handful of load-bearing claims are wrong (Tom Select's licence, `window.Admin`'s member list, whether twig-extensions ships a flash template, where the flash template is rendered); (3) a few dimensions the brief asked for were only grazed — the Symfony form-theme base evaluation, Symfony stateless CSRF interplay with `fetch`-based flows, i18n/RTL, the login page, and — most valuable — a real app to validate "drop-in" against, which exists on this machine (`APP/`, Sonata 4.43.0 + the user's Mongo fork 5.2.2 + ORM bundle + Encore + ux-autocomplete + stateless CSRF + 17 `templates/bundles/SonataAdminBundle/` overrides).
